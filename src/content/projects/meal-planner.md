## The problem

I was throwing away about a third of the vegetables I bought. Recipe apps start from a recipe
and send you shopping; I wanted the opposite: start from the fridge.

## What I did

- Interviewed five friends about how they decide what to cook on a weeknight.
- Built a simple inventory where you add items by typing or scanning a receipt.
- Wrote a scoring rule that favors meals using items closest to their expiry date.
- Tested it on myself for six weeks, adjusting the rules every Sunday.

## The result

Food waste dropped from roughly 30% to under 10% over the six weeks. Two of the friends I
interviewed still use it.

## What I'd do differently

Start with the receipt scanner. Typing items in by hand was the step everyone skipped.
