## The problem

The food bank tracked 120 volunteers in a spreadsheet that three coordinators edited at once.
Shifts were double-booked or left empty, and nobody noticed until the morning of.

## What I did

- Mapped how coordinators actually used the sheet before changing anything.
- Built a dashboard that highlights understaffed shifts for the next two weeks.
- Added a weekly email summary so coordinators didn't have to check it daily.

## The result

Empty shifts fell from about eight a month to two. Coordinators said the weekly email was the
part they valued most.
