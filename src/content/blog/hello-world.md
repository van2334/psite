description: A résumé lists what I did. I wanted somewhere to show how I think, and what I do on weekends.
date: 2026-11-09

A résumé is good at saying *what* I did. It's bad at saying *how* I think, and it has no room
at all for the things that make me who I am outside of work.

So this site has three parts:

- **Work**: a few projects, each written up as a short case study: the problem, what I did, and
  what happened.
- **Writing**: notes like this one, on whatever I'm learning.
- **About**: the longer story, including the cooking and the running.

## What I'll write about

Mostly two things: what I'm learning at work, and the side projects that come out of my
hobbies. The meal planner on the work page started because I was tired of throwing out
spinach.

If any of it is useful or interesting, I'd love to hear from you.
